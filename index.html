Flip It

Flip It is a self-sustaining coin-flip primitive on NEAR testnet. No tilted odds, just luck and fun. Each bet uses a future drand round verified on NEAR. Network fees apply.

Win, and you get double your stake back before a 1% winning payout fee. Lose, and your stake goes into the bank, which pays future winners.

The 1% fee on winning payouts stays in the bank, keeping its balance playable for the players who come next. Your net return is 1.98×, including your original stake. Older bets keep their original 2× payout.

A single bid is capped at 1% of the free bank with payouts reserved before a bet is accepted, and anyone can pledge to the bank to raise the limit for everyone.

Built for the NEAR hackathon, for the fun of it.
