Flip Fun

Flip Fun is a self-sustaining coin-flip primitive built to be a never-ending game. Every flip is a true 50/50: no fees, no house edge, no tilted odds, just luck.

Win, and you get double your stake back. Lose, and your stake goes into the bank, which pays future winners and keeps the game always open.

A single bid is capped at 5% of the bank so it can never be drained in one go, and anyone can pledge to the bank to raise the limit for everyone.

Built for the NEAR hackathon, for the fun of it.
