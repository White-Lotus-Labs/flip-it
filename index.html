Flip Fun

Flip Fun is a coin-flip game running on NEAR testnet. Each bet uses a future drand round verified on NEAR. Network fees apply.

Win, and you get double your stake back. Lose, and your stake goes into the bank, which pays future winners and helps fund future flips.

A single bid is capped at 1% of the free bank with payouts reserved before a bet is accepted, and anyone can pledge to the bank to raise the limit for everyone.

Built for the NEAR hackathon, for the fun of it.
